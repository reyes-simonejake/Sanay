# Sanay — 5-minute pitch

**One line:** *Practice the call. Sound clear on the real one.* / *Mag-practice muna. Sanay ka na sa totoong tawag.*
**Bucket 04 — Most Useful for the PH Community.** Judged on Impact 30% · Autonomy 25% · Craft 25% · Execution 20%.

The rule for the whole pitch: **show, don't tell.** Two thirds of the time is the live product.

---

| Time | Part | Goal |

## The script

### 1. The moment (0:00–0:35)
> "Picture a fresh graduate in a small room. In ten minutes: her first call center interview, in English.
> Outside: a tricycle, the neighbor's karaoke, a rooster. She has a cheap headset, and nobody to practice with.
> She knows the answers in Filipino. Her nerves won't let them out in English.
> That's not a skills problem. That's a **practice** problem. And it's how millions of us go into the calls that change our lives."

*(Use a real person if you have one: yourself, a friend, a cousin. A true story beats any number. Don't invent one.)*

### 2. Sanay in one line (0:35–0:55)
> "Sanay is a free practice partner for the conversations that matter: job interviews, call center hiring, visa interviews, scholarships.
> **Practice the call. Sound clear on the real one.** Libre. Sa phone mo. Walang account."

### 3. Live demo (0:55–3:35)

**Beat A: Practice interview (1:30).** Open *Account → Practice interview*.
1. Pick **Feminine voice** → **Start**.
2. The interviewer asks *"What kind of interview are you preparing for?"* → say **"A call center job."**
3. Answer **question 1** well (rehearsed, ~20 s). Point at the **live captions**: *"It hears me as I speak."*
4. Answer **question 2** a bit badly on purpose: short, with "parang… ano…". The partner asks *"Can you tell me a little more?"* → *"Just like a real interviewer."*
5. Tap **End practice** → results: **Ready score**, *What to work on* (the "parang" advice), plain checklist, **replay** your answer, **Hear the feedback**.
> "It ran the whole interview by itself: asked, listened, followed up, scored, and coached. No teacher needed."

**Beat B: Say it in English (0:25).** Tap the mic, say: *"Pasensya na po, ichecheck ko lang kung nasaan na yung order ninyo."*
> "Think in Filipino, say it in clear English, in your own voice."

**Beat C: Pronunciation (0:20).** Open **F / P**, Step 1: it plays a word, tap **fan** or **pan**.
> "Sounds we mix up, F and P, V and B, trained in two minutes, explained in Taglish."

**Beat D: The real call (0:25).** Desktop app + a call (Meet). Play the **karaoke / tricycle clip** beside you → tap the orb → silence.
> "And on the real call, Sanay removes the noise around you. Live."
*(Optional, if time: switch to the feminine/masculine voice → the one-time fair-use notice appears.)*

### 4. Why it matters (3:35–4:20)
> "**Impact:** it's for the people who need it most: job seekers, BPO applicants, OFWs, students. Free, works in Chrome on a budget Android, in English and natural Filipino, no account.
> **Autonomy:** the practice partner runs the whole interview on its own: it chooses the questions, listens, follows up, scores against clear rules, and coaches.
> **Craft:** every screen is built for people who aren't techy: big buttons, plain words, a back button everywhere.
> **Execution:** what you just saw is live, and tested end to end."

### 5. Honest build + next (4:20–4:45)
> "Full honesty: the noise-cleanup engine was made before this event. Here we built the practice interview, the scoring and coaching, Say it in English, the pronunciation coach, and the Filipino interface.
> Next: Filipino subtitles of the other person during real calls, and free access for every PESO office and school."

### 6. Close (4:45–5:00)
> "Before the call that changes your life, you shouldn't have to practice alone.
> **Mag-practice muna. Sanay ka na.** Thank you."

## Rules while presenting
- **Speak to the judges, not the screen.** Tell them what to look at ("Look at the score…").
- **Never say "AI model", "API", "pipeline".** Say "practice partner", "it listens", "your score".
- **Don't read the script.** Learn the 6 headings and the first sentence of each.
- **If something stalls, narrate and move on.** "It's waiting for my answer; I'll tap I'm done." The backup buttons are part of the product.
- **Stop at 5:00.** Cut Beat C first, then the optional voice switch.

## Before going on stage (checklist)
- [ ] The site open in **Chrome**, signed in with the sample account, tabs ready: *Practice interview*, *Say it in English*, *Pronunciation*.
- [ ] Mic allowed in Chrome; **earphones** in (so the interviewer's voice doesn't enter the mic).
- [ ] Venue Wi-Fi tested; **phone hotspot** ready (speech recognition and translation need internet).
- [ ] Desktop app running, VB-Cable set as the mic in Meet; noise clip and speaker tested; AI voice server warmed up if you'll show it.
- [ ] Language set to **English** for the judges (switch to Filipino once, to show it, if there's time).
- [ ] **Recorded backup video** of the full demo, one click away.
- [ ] Close other apps: the laptop is short on memory.

## If something goes wrong
| Problem | Do this |
|---|---|
| Interviewer doesn't hear you | Tap **I'm done**; or at the start, tap **Call center / BPO** instead of saying it |
| No internet | Switch to the hotspot; if still down, play the backup video for Beats A–C and do Beat D live (it works offline) |
| Translation fails | Type the sentence in the box and tap **Make it English** |
| Desktop app or noise demo fails | Skip Beat D: "On the real call it removes the noise; you can try it on our site's mic test." |

## Likely judge questions (short answers)
- **"Is it just a chatbot?"** — No. It's a full practice session with voice, scoring and coaching, made for one job: getting Filipinos ready for the calls that matter.
- **"How do you score?"** — Clear rules anyone can check: did you cover the key points, length, pace, filler words like "parang", pauses, how quickly you started. Each one is explained on screen.
- **"Privacy?"** — Calls: processed on your own computer. Practice: Chrome's speech recognition sends audio to Google to write down your words, and we say so on screen. Sanay saves nothing.
- **"Can the voice option be misused?"** — Preset voices only, no cloning, a one-time fair-use notice and a reminder on live calls; scores never judge the voice.
- **"Why would people use this over free YouTube tips?"** — YouTube can't listen to you. Sanay makes you say it out loud and tells you what to fix.
- **"How will you reach users?"** — Free, no account, a link that works on any phone; partner with PESO offices, schools and pre-departure seminars.
- **"What did you build here?"** — See part 5. Be exact.

## How to practice the pitch (today)
1. **Run 1 (read it):** read aloud once with a timer. Note where you go over.
2. **Runs 2–3 (headings only):** speak from the 6 headings. Record yourself on your phone; listen once.
3. **Run 4 (with the demo):** full run with the live product, timer visible. Cut until it's 4:45.
4. **Run 5 (break it):** have someone switch off Wi-Fi mid-demo; practice the recovery line.
5. **Last run:** in front of one person who doesn't know Sanay. Ask: "What is it, and who is it for?" If they can't answer in one sentence, fix part 2.
